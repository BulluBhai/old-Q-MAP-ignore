How to read this
  - A historical rank is a description of the past, not evidence that a moving average will work in future.
  - Many candidates (periods and MA types) are tested and the best are shown, so some will look good by chance. p-values are NOT corrected for this (multiple comparisons) and must not be read as proof.
  - Neighbouring MA periods are almost identical, so the Top-N list may show one finding several times.
  - Forward windows longer than the primary window overlap each other, so those statistics are not independent observations.
  - Success is defined by the sign of the forward return (price higher after Support, lower after Resistance). It is not compared with the market's general drift.
  - Forward Return (Directed IR) methodology is PENDING OWNER REVIEW. The V2 formula is kept unchanged and is documented in the README.
  - The official affinity weight scheme is still undecided. The four V2 weights are kept in their original proportions and normalised.
  - Out-of-sample samples can be small. Where the OOS sample is below the minimum, consistency is reported as insufficient evidence.
  - These results describe historical data only. They do not predict or guarantee future price behaviour or future performance of any reference. This is a research tool, not a trading signal or recommendation.
  - Full tables: MA_Affinity_Candidates and MA_Affinity_Interactions (CSV), the Excel workbook and the PDF.
